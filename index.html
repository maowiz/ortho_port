Biography

A Passion for Healing Through Precision

Prof. Dr. Naseer Ahmad Chaudhary is one of South Punjab's senior orthopaedic surgeons, currently serving as Professor and Head of the Orthopaedic Department at Bahawal Victoria Hospital (BVH), Bahawalpur, and as Visiting Professor at Quaid-e-Azam Medical College, Bahawalpur.

He completed his MBBS at Allama Iqbal Medical College (AIMC), Lahore, and went on to earn the FCPS in Orthopaedic Surgery from the College of Physicians & Surgeons Pakistan. He is a Member of AO Trauma (Switzerland) and holds the CHPE (UK) qualification in health professions education.

He previously served as Head of Orthopaedics at Sheikh Zayed Medical College / Hospital, Rahim Yar Khan, where he led both clinical services and the training of young surgeons.

His clinical approach rests on three principles: accurate diagnosis, the right treatment with surgery only when genuinely needed, and complete support through rehabilitation and recovery.
